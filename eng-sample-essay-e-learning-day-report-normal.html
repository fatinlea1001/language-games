SPM Writing Part 3 · Original E-Learning Day Report Task

Exam question

Recently, your school organised an E-Learning Day to encourage the students to use technology to learn.

As the secretary of the ICT club, you have been asked to write a report about the event.

In your report, you should include:

  • what activities were carried out during the event
  • why it was successful / unsuccessful
  • how to improve the event in the future

Write your report.

Complete sample report · NORMAL
