Original Report Task

Exam question

You recently attended a Leadership Seminar organised by your school.

As the Secretary of the Prefects' Board, write a report to your principal about the event.

In your report, you should include:

  • what activities were carried out during the seminar
  • why it was successful / unsuccessful
  • how to improve the event in the future
Complete sample report · EASY
To: Puan Farah Ismail, Principal of SMK Seri Harmoni
From: Nur Aina Hassan, Secretary of the Prefects' Board
Subject: Report on the Leadership Seminar

Introduction

This report is about the Leadership Seminar organised by our school. This report will focus on the activities carried out during the seminar, why it was successful and how to improve the event in the future.

Activities During the Seminar

To begin with, there were several activities during the seminar. One activity was a team-building game. Each group had to build a tower using paper. This activity helped teamwork among the prefects. Another activity was a public-speaking session. Each prefect gave a short speech in front of the group. The teachers gave to help us speak .

Reasons for Success

Furthermore, there were several reasons the seminar was successful. One reason was that the prefects worked well together. They shared ideas when solving problems. As a result, every group completed its task. Another reason was that the teachers gave clear instructions. They showed us what to do before each activity. Therefore, everyone could join the activities without difficulty.

Suggestions for Improvement

Finally, there are several ways to improve the seminar. One suggestion is to more time for the speaking session. Some prefects did not have enough time to practise. A longer session would help them speak with greater confidence. Another suggestion is to provide more drinking water. The could place water bottles near the hall. This would help everyone stay comfortable.

Conclusion

To conclude, the Leadership Seminar was successful and meaningful. Hence, it is hoped that this report will bring positive changes.
