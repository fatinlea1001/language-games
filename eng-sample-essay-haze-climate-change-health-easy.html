To begin with, haze and climate change can affect people's health in several ways. One effect is that to haze can make breathing difficult. Another effect is that very hot weather can make people lose too much water. When the temperature rises, people sweat more and may feel weak or dizzy. As a result, they may become sick if they do not rest and drink enough water.
