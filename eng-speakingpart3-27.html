Speaking Part 3 · Discussion Question

Why do some people commit crimes?

Choose any five factors from the mind map. Explain how each might contribute to crime.

💡 Speaking tip: Each paragraph begins with a transition, followed by an explanation, an example and a result. / Tip bertutur: Setiap perenggan bermula dengan penanda wacana, diikuti huraian, contoh dan kesan.

🔎 Speaking tip: Explain how each factor might contribute to crime, give an example and describe a possible result or solution. / Tip bertutur: Terangkan bagaimana setiap faktor mungkin menyumbang kepada jenayah, berikan contoh dan jelaskan kesan atau penyelesaian.

🧭 Remember: These are possible risk factors, not predictions. Most people experiencing hardship or family difficulties do not commit crimes. / Ingat: Ini merupakan faktor risiko yang mungkin berlaku, bukan ramalan tentang seseorang.
