Your class has been talking about cyberbullying and your teacher has asked you to write an essay about it.

Cyberbullying on Social Media

Social media helps teenagers stay connected, yet it can also become a place where hurtful behaviour spreads quickly. In particular, cyberbullying can affect a student's confidence even after they log off. Therefore, understanding its causes and taking action are important ways to protect young people online.
