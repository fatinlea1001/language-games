Target Supermarket Parit Raja : A Review

Over the years, I have visited supermarkets to help my family purchase household necessities. While some supermarkets offer a wide selection of products, others attract customers with prices. Recently, I visited Target Supermarket in Parit Raja, Johor, and enjoyed a pleasant shopping experience. In this review, I will explain how frequently I visit the supermarket and describe the items I normally purchase.

To begin with, I visit Target Supermarket once or twice a week, particularly on Friday evenings. Its location allows my family to buy groceries without travelling far from home. I usually accompany my mother to purchase items for the coming week. Before leaving home, we prepare a shopping list and set a spending limit. This list is because it reminds us of important items, especially when the supermarket is crowded. For instance, we can find what we need without spending too much time looking around. , our shopping trips are more organised and less stressful.

Furthermore, I frequently buy fresh vegetables, eggs, milk, bread and rice at the supermarket. I prefer choosing fresh products myself because I can check their quality before buying them. For example, I usually for fresh fruits instead of packaged sweets for my school snacks. I also compare prices between similar products to make sure they suit our budget. Whenever there are offers, I check whether the products are worth buying. Following our shopping list also helps unnecessary purchases. As a result, we can get quality products while avoiding overspending.

In conclusion, I would certainly recommend online payment at Target Supermarket to other customers. This is because it offers a convenient way to pay, reduces the need to carry cash and can make the checkout process faster. I particularly being able to check the payment amount and keep a digital record of my spending. Hence, I hope more shoppers will consider using online payment for a smoother shopping experience.
