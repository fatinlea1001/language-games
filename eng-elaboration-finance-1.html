The Paragraph Makeover

Theme: Health & Environment

Rewrite the paragraph below in approximately 80–100 words. Make the ideas flow naturally and explain why the issue matters.

People should reduce plastic waste. Plastic waste is bad for the environment. For example, people can use reusable bags. Reusable bags are good. This will help the environment. The environment is important for everyone.

Your mission

  • Develop the main idea instead of repeating it.
  • Include a specific, relevant example.
  • Connect the example to its wider impact.
  • Vary sentence structures and vocabulary.
  • Finish with a meaningful concluding thought.

Model paragraph

Reducing plastic waste is essential to protecting the environment, as discarded plastic can pollute waterways and threaten wildlife. One practical way to address this problem is to replace single-use plastic bags with reusable alternatives when shopping. If more people adopt this habit, fewer plastic bags will end up in landfills or natural habitats. Although individual actions may seem insignificant, collective efforts can make a meaningful difference. By making sustainable choices part of daily life, communities can help preserve the environment for future generations.
