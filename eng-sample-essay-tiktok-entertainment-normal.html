TikTok: Entertainment at Our Fingertips

Written by: Ethan Wong

In recent years, entertainment applications such as TikTok have gained increasing attention from people of all ages. Whether we realise it or not, this topic plays an important role in our daily lives. This article will discuss my favourite entertainment application, its impact on my daily routine and the reasons teenagers spend too much time on their gadgets.

To begin with, there is a wide range of entertainment applications available to young people. One application I particularly enjoy is TikTok. It offers short videos covering comedy, music, cooking and educational tips. I normally browse it after completing homework rather than during study time. For instance, I enjoy watching creative editing ideas and simple science demonstrations. These clips make TikTok an entertaining way to spend a short break.

For instance, someone who opens TikTok for five minutes may still be scrolling an hour later. Another reason is the pressure to follow new trends and respond to friends. Therefore, parents and schools should work together to promote sensible gadget use and balanced daily routines.
