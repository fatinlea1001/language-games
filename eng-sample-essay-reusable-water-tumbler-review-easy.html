Reusable Water Tumbler : A Review

Over the years, I have tried to use fewer plastic items in my daily life. Some changes were difficult, while others were surprisingly easy. Recently, I bought a reusable water tumbler to bring to school. In this review, I will describe the product and explain its best .

To begin with, my tumbler is a stainless steel bottle that I use every day. It has a blue body, a tight lid and a handle for carrying it. I fill it with water before leaving home each morning. For example, I bring it to school and refill it at the water dispenser during recess. It is a to buying plastic water bottles. I can wash it and use it again to resources instead of buying a new bottle. As a result, it is a choice for students like me.

Furthermore, the best feature of my tumbler is that it keeps water cold for hours. It has two layers of steel that help keep the water cool. For example, my drink is still cold when I take a break after PE class. The lid also closes tightly, so I do not worry about water spilling into my bag. Since I bring my own drink, I no longer need to bottled water at school. This helps me plastic waste each day. As a result, I enjoy using my tumbler and save some money too.

In conclusion, I would recommend this reusable water tumbler to others. This is because it is easy to carry, keeps drinks cold and helps reduce plastic waste. I enjoy using it at school and at home. Hence, I hope more people will choose reusable bottles to protect the environment.
