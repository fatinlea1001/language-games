Exam question

Your class has been talking about cyberbullying and your teacher has asked you to write an essay about it.

In your essay, you should write about:

  • one example of cyberbullying on social media
  • why online bullying happens
  • ways students can stop cyberbullying

Use all the notes above and give reasons for your point of view.

Cyberbullying on Social Media

Nowadays, many students use social media to share photos and talk with friends. However, some people use these platforms to hurt others. Therefore, it is important to understand what cyberbullying looks like, why it happens and how students can stop it.

Firstly, one example of cyberbullying on social media is posting cruel comments under a student's photo. Some bullies write hurtful words about the student's to them in front of others.
