Question 2 · Original Exam Task

Exam question

Your class has been discussing the usage of electronic reading materials among teenagers. Your teacher has asked you to write an essay on it.

In your essay, you should write about:

  • benefits of reading e-books
  • how school can promote the habit of reading e-books among teenagers
  • teenagers’ preference in future; to read e-books or paper books? Why?

Use all the notes above and give reasons for your point of view.

Complete sample answer · EASY

Reading E-books Among Teenagers

Reading plays an important role in teenagers' lives. Nowadays, many students read stories and find information using e-books. Therefore, it is useful to understand the of e-books and how schools can teenagers to read them.

The of reading e-books include the convenience they offer teenagers. For example, students can store many titles on one phone without carrying several heavy books. They can also read a story while waiting for the school bus. Another benefit is that e-books offer a wide of reading materials. For instance, readers can choose from stories, magazines and books in the same app. As a result, teenagers can topics that match their interests.

Schools can promote the habit of reading e-books among teenagers by organising weekly e-book reading sessions. During the session, teachers can give students time to read stories in class. Over time, this can students to read more . In addition, schools can organise a reading challenge. For example, each class can record how many e-books its students finish in a month. As a reward, students who take part can receive certificates, which may others to join.

Teenagers’ preference in the future may shift towards e-books rather than paper books. This is because many teenagers already use phones and can read without buying another device. For instance, they can download a novel at home instead of travelling to a bookshop. Besides, they can adjust the text size to make reading more comfortable. However, paper books may still to teenagers who want a break from screens.

In conclusion, e-books are practical and offer many reading choices. Therefore, schools should a love of reading through interesting activities. Although paper books are still popular, I believe more teenagers will prefer e-books in the future.
