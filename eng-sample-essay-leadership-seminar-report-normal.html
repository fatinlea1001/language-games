Original Report Task

Exam question

You recently attended a Leadership Seminar organised by your school.

As the Secretary of the Prefects' Board, write a report to your principal about the event.

In your report, you should include:

  • what activities were carried out during the seminar
  • why it was successful / unsuccessful
  • how to improve the event in the future
Complete sample report · NORMAL
To: Puan Farah Ismail, Principal of SMK Seri Harmoni
From: Daniel Hakim, Secretary of the Prefects' Board
Subject: Report on the Leadership Seminar

Introduction

This report gives an overview of the recent Leadership Seminar. It was organised by the school and was held in the school hall. This report will focus on the activities carried out during the seminar, the reasons for its success and ways to improve the event in the future.

Activities During the Seminar

To begin with, the seminar featured several activities designed to develop leadership skills. One activity was a team-building challenge in which prefects constructed a tower using paper. Each group had to assign roles and agree on a suitable design within a limited time. This exercise helped and decision-making skills. Another activity was a public-speaking workshop. delivered short speeches on their duties as prefects and received from teachers. Consequently, they became more confident when addressing an audience.

Reasons for Success

Furthermore, several factors contributed to the success of the seminar. One reason was the clear provided by the teachers. Before each task, they demonstrated the steps and answered questions from participants. As a result, the activities proceeded smoothly with little confusion. Another reason was the shown by the prefects throughout the programme. More experienced prefects encouraged their juniors to share ideas and take on different roles. This supportive enabled everyone to contribute to their group's success.

Suggestions for Improvement

Finally, several improvements could make future seminars more effective. One suggestion is to additional time for the public-speaking workshop. A longer session would allow teachers to offer individual feedback and give participants a second opportunity to present. As a result, prefects could learn from their mistakes and improve their speaking skills. Another suggestion is to provide more drinking water during the programme. Water stations could be placed near the hall so that participants can refill their bottles during breaks. This would help them remain comfortable and focused throughout the seminar.

Conclusion

Overall, the Leadership Seminar proved to be both enjoyable and educational. It is hoped that similar activities will be held regularly.
