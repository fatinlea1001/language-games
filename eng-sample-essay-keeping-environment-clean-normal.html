Protecting Our Environment and Marine Life

In recent years, keeping the environment clean has gained increasing attention from people of all ages. Whether we realise it or not, this topic plays an important role in our daily lives. This essay will discuss one way to protect our surroundings, the effects of pollution and how a clean environment benefits sea creatures.

To begin with, there are ways to keep our environment clean. One useful method is to organise regular community clean-up activities. Working together makes it easier to remove litter from public spaces before it reaches drains and rivers. Therefore, even a simple activity can encourage the community to take better care of shared spaces.

Furthermore, pollution has several harmful effects on people and the natural world. One effect is an increase in breathing problems caused by polluted air. Smoke from vehicles and open burning can irritate people's lungs, especially if they already have health problems. For example, students may find outdoor sports uncomfortable when the air is filled with smoke. Another effect is the damage caused to rivers and beaches. Finally, a clean environment offers several important benefits to sea creatures. One benefit is that fish and turtles can in cleaner seas. Clean water reduces their risk of injury from plastic and other harmful waste. For instance, turtles are less likely to mistake floating plastic bags for food. Another benefit is that clean seas support healthier food sources. When coral reefs and sea plants remain healthy, smaller creatures can survive and provide food for larger ones. As a result, protecting the sea helps different species survive and keeps ocean life in balance.

In conclusion, protecting our environment is for both human health and the survival of sea creatures. It is hoped that more teenagers will take responsibility for reducing pollution in their communities. Therefore, schools and local authorities should work together to organise clean-up programmes and promote responsible waste disposal.
