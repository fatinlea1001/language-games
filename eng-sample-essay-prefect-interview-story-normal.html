The Prefect Interview

The notice on the school bulletin board immediately caught my attention. Applications were open for students who wished to become school prefects. Although I had always the prefects for their leadership skills, I had never imagined becoming one myself. After some encouragement from my friends, I decided to attend the prefect selection interview. Little did I know that preparing for it would be one of my greatest challenges.

To begin with, I spent two weeks preparing for the interview. I researched the of a prefect and prepared answers to common interview questions. Every evening, I practised speaking in front of a mirror to improve my confidence. My older sister also helped by conducting mock interviews at home. "Why do you think you deserve this position?" she asked during one practice session. Her questions encouraged me to think carefully about my strengths and weaknesses.

Nevertheless, the preparation was more challenging than I had expected. I had to revise for an important examination while completing my school assignments. I was afraid that the lack of time would my progress. To make matters worse, I experienced a when I suddenly forgot my answers during a mock interview. Feeling embarrassed, I almost gave up. However, my sister reminded me that making mistakes was part of learning. With determination and , I continued practising until I could answer questions more confidently.

Eventually, the interview day arrived. My heart raced as I entered the room and faced three teachers. Although I was nervous, I reminded myself to remain calm. One interviewer asked me what qualities a good prefect should possess. "A good prefect should be responsible, fair and willing to help others," I replied confidently. I also shared my experiences of helping teachers organise school activities. As the interview continued, my nervousness gradually disappeared, and I began to enjoy the conversation.

A week later, the results were announced during the morning assembly. I stood among my classmates, listening anxiously as the teacher read out the names of the successful candidates. Suddenly, I heard my name! For a moment, I stood completely still before my friends began cheering. A huge smile spread across my face as I walked towards the stage to receive my prefect badge. I felt incredibly proud, knowing that I had my fears and achieved something meaningful. At that moment, I knew all my hard work had been worth it.
