PRACTICE 4

You attended a workshop on how to study effectively. As the secretary of your school council, you have been asked to write a report for the principal.

In your report, you must answer these questions:

  • when was the workshop?
  • what were the two tips shared during the workshop?
  • what were the challenges when applying the tips?

Write your report.

Complete sample report · NORMAL
To: The Principal
From: Daniel Lim, Secretary of the School Council
Subject: Report on the Workshop on How to Study Effectively

Introduction

This report provides an overview of the workshop on effective study attended by Form Five students. The programme was organised to help students prepare more confidently for their examinations. It outlines the date and details of the workshop, two useful techniques presented by the speaker and the challenges of putting them into practice.

When the Workshop Was Held

To begin with, the study skills workshop was held on Friday, 16 October 2026, from 9.00 a.m. to 12.00 noon. The session took place in the school hall and was organised by the school counselling unit. A guest speaker guided Form Five students through practical revision activities. For instance, worked in small groups to prepare a weekly timetable. They also discussed common mistakes that can make revision less effective. , the workshop gave students a clearer understanding of how to manage their study time.

Two Tips Shared at the Workshop

Furthermore, the speaker introduced two for studying more effectively. One tip was to challenging subjects when preparing a weekly revision schedule. By giving difficult topics more attention, students can make steady progress instead of revising only their favourite subjects. For example, a student who struggles with Mathematics could set aside three short practice sessions each week. Another tip was to use active recall by answering questions without looking at notes. This technique can deeper understanding because students must retrieve information from memory. As a result, they can identify weaknesses and review the topics that need more practice.

Challenges When Applying the Tips

Nevertheless, students may encounter several difficulties when applying these techniques. One challenge is maintaining a study schedule while managing homework and family . Unexpected activities can interrupt planned revision sessions and cause students to fall behind. For example, a student might miss an evening session after attending an extra class. Another challenge is avoiding distractions during active-recall practice. Frequent phone can and make it difficult to finish practice questions. Therefore, setting realistic study targets and keeping electronic devices away can make these strategies easier to follow.

Conclusion

Overall, the workshop provided valuable guidance on planning revision and checking understanding through active recall. It is hoped that more students will apply these strategies consistently in preparation for their examinations. Accordingly, the School Council recommends short follow-up sessions so that teachers can help students overcome difficulties and develop effective study routines.
