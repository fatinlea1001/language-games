SPM Writing Part 3 · Original School Library Survey Task

Exam question

Recently, the librarians in your school conducted a survey about the school library. Your teacher has assigned you to write a report on the findings.

In your report, you must answer these questions:

  • what are the facilities provided by the library?
  • what is the most popular activity carried out in the library?
  • what should be upgraded at the library?

Write your report.

Complete sample report · EASY
To: Puan Rohana Ismail, Principal of SMK Seri Wawasan
From: Nur Aqeela, Secretary of the Library Club
Subject: Report on the School Library Survey

Introduction

This report is about a of 100 students on our school library conducted by the librarians. This report will focus on the provided, the most popular activity and what should be .

Facilities Provided by the Library

To begin with, our library provides several useful facilities. It has long study tables and comfortable chairs for students. There are also bookshelves filled with storybooks and books. For example, students can use the tables to complete homework after class. The library also has computers that online learning. As a result, students have a suitable place to read and study.

Most Popular Activity in the Library

In addition, the survey showed that reading storybooks was the most popular activity in the library. Out of 100 students, 60 chose reading storybooks as their favourite activity. They enjoyed reading interesting stories during recess and after school. For example, many students borrowed mystery and adventure books from the reading corner. Other students preferred doing homework or searching for information. Therefore, the survey showed that students visited the library mainly to enjoy reading.

Suggested Upgrades for the Library

Finally, there are several facilities that should be upgraded. One is the library computers because some are old and slow. New computers would help students search for information more easily. For example, students could use them to prepare school projects without waiting too long. Another improvement is to replace broken chairs and add brighter lights. The school should these changes to make the library safer and more comfortable. As a result, more students may want to spend time there.

Conclusion

To conclude, the library survey was useful and meaningful. Hence, it is hoped that this report will help the school improve the library.
