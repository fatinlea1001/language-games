The best article will be published in our teen magazine next month.

Write your article.

Complete sample article · EASY

Teenagers and Pets

Written by: Your Name

Nowadays, many teenagers enjoy keeping pets at home. Pets can bring happiness, but they also need love and care. In this article, I will share my choice of pet, ways to care for it and how pets teach responsibility.

To begin with, there are many animals that people can keep as pets. One animal I would choose is a cat. Cats are playful and can keep me company after school. For example, I could spend time playing with my cat in the living room.

Furthermore, there are several ways to show love and care for a pet. One way is to give my cat healthy food and clean water every day. This helps it stay strong and healthy. Another way is to keep its sleeping area clean. For example, I would wash its blanket and clean the litter box .

Finally, there are several ways owning a pet can make teenagers more . One way is by teaching them to follow a daily . For example, they must feed their pets before leaving for school. Another way is by teenagers to become pet owners. They learn to notice when a pet is sick and ask an adult for help.

In conclusion, cats can be wonderful pets, but looking after them takes effort. Therefore, teenagers should only keep a pet if they are ready to care for it every day.
