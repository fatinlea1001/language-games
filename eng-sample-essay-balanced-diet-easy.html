GUIDED PRACTICE 1

Your class has been talking about a balanced diet, and your teacher has asked you to write an essay about it.

In your essay, you should:

  • state the food that you consider healthy
  • explain the effect of eating unhealthy food
  • suggest ways to stay healthy

Use all the notes above and give reasons for your point of view.

Write your essay in 125 - 150 words in an appropriate style.

The Importance of a Balanced Diet

Nowadays, many teenagers enjoy eating different types of food every day. However, choosing the right food is important because it helps our bodies stay strong. This essay will discuss healthy food, the effects of unhealthy food and ways to stay healthy.

To begin with, there are several types of food that I consider healthy. One type is fresh fruit and vegetables. They contain vitamins and help our bodies fight illness. For example, I enjoy eating bananas and spinach with my meals. Another choice is fish and eggs. These foods provide protein, which helps our bodies grow and repair muscles. As a result, eating them regularly can help us feel stronger.

In addition, eating unhealthy food too often can cause several problems. One effect is weight gain. This is because fast food and sweet drinks often contain a lot of . For example, having fried chicken and sugary drinks every day may lead to weight gain over time. Another effect is feeling tired during the day. Foods high in sugar can give us quick energy, but this feeling does not always last. As a result, students may find it harder to in class.

Finally, there are several simple ways to stay healthy. One way is to eat balanced meals every day. We should include vegetables, some protein and rice or other grains in suitable amounts. For example, we can choose water instead of sweet drinks at lunch. Another way is to exercise regularly. We can walk, cycle or play badminton for fun. By doing so, we can stay active and improve our health.

In conclusion, eating healthy food and avoiding too much junk food can help us live better lives. Hence, parents and schools should encourage teenagers to make healthier choices every day.
