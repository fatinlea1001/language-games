You saw this notice on the bulletin board in your class.

We'd like to hear from you.

Our school canteen has recently been upgraded. Write and tell us your thoughts.

In your review, you should include:

  • what do you like the most about the canteen?
  • do you think it is convenient for pupils? Why?
  • what other improvements should be added? Give some ideas.

The New School Canteen

Over the years, I have visited various school canteens, and each offered a different dining experience. Although many provided decent food, only a few were comfortable enough to leave a lasting impression on me. Recently, I visited our newly upgraded school canteen and was impressed by its new . In this review, I will discuss my favourite feature, consider its convenience and suggest further improvements.

To begin with, there is one feature I particularly appreciate about the upgraded school canteen. My favourite feature is its and well-organised dining area. The tables are arranged with enough room between them, making it easier for pupils to move around during the busy recess period. For instance, my classmates and I can find seats together without blocking the walkways or disturbing others. This thoughtful helps the problem of crowded walkways, creating a calmer and more welcoming . Consequently, pupils can enjoy their meals without feeling unnecessarily rushed.

Furthermore, there is a practical reason why I consider the upgraded canteen convenient for pupils. One significant improvement is the clearer arrangement of food and drink counters. This can smoother queues because pupils know where to order and collect their meals. For example, those buying rice dishes can line up separately from pupils purchasing drinks. The clear signs also reduce confusion, especially when many students arrive at the same time. As a result, pupils can buy their food more and return to class on time.

Finally, there are several improvements that could make the new school canteen even better. One suggestion is to provide a wider selection of and meals. This would help pupils make healthier choices without spending too much money. For instance, the canteen could offer fruit, vegetable dishes and reasonably priced set meals. Another suggestion is to install more rubbish and recycling bins near the dining area. Clear labels on these bins could the importance of keeping shared spaces clean. Ultimately, these changes would make the canteen healthier and more pleasant for everyone.

In conclusion, I would recommend the upgraded school canteen to other pupils. This is because it is comfortable and convenient, although a few improvements would make it even better. I enjoyed having meals there with my friends and appreciated the pleasant . Hence, I hope the school will continue improving its food choices and cleanliness.
