Exam question

Your class has been talking about learning a musical instrument. Your teacher has asked you to write an essay about it.

In your essay, you should:

  • name the musical instrument
  • explain why you want to learn it
  • discuss how music helps you express yourself

Use all the notes above and give reasons for your point of view.

Write your essay in 125 - 150 words in an appropriate style.

Complete sample essay · EASY

Learning to Play the Piano

Nowadays, many people are talking about learning musical instruments. This topic plays an important role in our daily lives. This essay will discuss the instrument I want to learn, why I want to learn it and how music helps me express myself.

To begin with, there are many musical instruments that students can learn. One instrument I want to learn is the piano. A piano has black and white keys that produce different notes. I would start by learning where the notes are before playing a simple tune. For example, I can practise a short birthday song with one hand. With regular practice, I hope to my goal of playing a whole song for my family.

In addition, there are several reasons why I want to learn the piano. One reason is that I enjoy listening to gentle piano music. It helps me relax when I feel tired after school. For example, I often listen to slow piano songs while resting at home. Another reason is that I want to play music for my family. I could practise a song for my mother's birthday and perform it at home. As a result, I would feel proud to share something I have worked hard to learn.

Finally, there are several ways music helps me express myself. One way is by showing my feelings without speaking. The sound of the piano can match my mood. For example, I may play a gentle tune after a difficult day. Another way is by sharing my happiness with others. I can play a cheerful song when my friends come to visit. As a result, music can and help my friends understand how I feel.

In conclusion, learning the piano is a fun way to enjoy music and share feelings. Hence, parents and schools should teenagers to try musical instruments and practise regularly.
