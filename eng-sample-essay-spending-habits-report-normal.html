SPM Writing Part 3 · Original Survey Report Task

Exam question

You recently conducted a survey among pupils in your school about their spending habits. The results showed that many pupils lack proper financial management skills.

As the secretary of the Consumer Club, you have been asked to write a report to your school principal.

In your report, you should include:

  • the purpose of the survey
  • the findings on pupils' spending habits
  • suggestions to improve pupils' financial awareness

Write your report.

Complete sample report · NORMAL
To: The Principal
From: Secretary, Consumer Club
Subject: Report on Pupils' Spending Habits

Introduction

This report provides an overview of the survey findings on pupils' spending habits and financial awareness. The survey was conducted by the Consumer Club among pupils in our school. This report examines the purpose of the survey, highlights the main spending patterns and recommends practical measures to improve financial management.

Purpose of the Survey

To begin with, there were two main of conducting the survey. One objective was to identify how pupils use their daily allowances. The focused on whether pupils spend their money on items or unnecessary . For instance, were asked how frequently they bought snacks and drinks during recess. Another objective was to assess pupils' ability to manage money independently. We wanted to determine whether they set savings goals or kept proper records of their . Consequently, the survey provided a clearer picture of their financial habits.

Findings of the Survey

Furthermore, the survey revealed two important findings about pupils' spending habits. One finding was that many pupils frequently purchased non- items. Some spent a large portion of their allowances on snacks and drinks, leaving funds for savings. For example, several admitted buying additional snacks simply because their friends did so. Another finding was that pupils rarely recorded their daily . Without tracking their , they struggled to necessary spending from avoidable spending. As a result, some found it difficult to manage their pocket money throughout the week.

Suggestions for Improvement

Finally, there are several practical measures the school could introduce to improve pupils' financial awareness. One recommendation is to organise short budgeting activities during lessons. Through these activities, pupils could learn to part of their weekly allowance to meals, savings and other necessities. For instance, they could practise planning a RM20 budget and reviewing their spending at the end of the week. Another recommendation is to conduct a financial workshop for pupils. During the session, participants could learn to distinguish between needs and wants while discussing ways to avoid unnecessary . Accordingly, these activities would equip pupils with the knowledge needed to make more responsible financial decisions.

Conclusion

Overall, the findings clearly indicate that many pupils need stronger budgeting habits and a better understanding of everyday spending. Based on these findings, it is recommended that the school provide regular financial activities to encourage responsible money management.
