Exam question

Your class has been discussing how helping others has affected them and your teacher has asked you to write an essay about being charitable.

In your essay, you should:

  • state an activity you do to help people
  • describe why you help others
  • explain how helping people can change a person
Complete sample essay · EASY

Helping Others Through Charity

Nowadays, helping others is an important part of our lives. Even small acts of kindness can make people happy. This essay will discuss an activity I do, why I help others and how helping people can change a person.

To begin with, there are many ways to help people in need. One activity I do is prepare food packs for poor families. I put bread, canned food and other basic items into bags with my friends. As a result, these families have some food to take home.

Furthermore, there are several reasons why I enjoy helping others. One reason is that I feel sad when I see families . I want to their worries by giving them useful . Another reason is that helping others makes me happy. Seeing people smile encourages me to continue .

Finally, there are several ways helping people can change a person. One change is that they become kinder and more understanding. For example, they learn to listen to people who face problems. Another change is that they become more . They learn to keep their promises and work well with other volunteers.

In conclusion, activities can benefit both the people receiving help and the volunteers. Therefore, everyone should try to help others whenever they can.

Each body paragraph matches the EASY Charity Elaboration Builder exactly. The introduction and conclusion follow the Part 2 lesson notes.
