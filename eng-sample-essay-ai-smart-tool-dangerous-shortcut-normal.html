AI: A Smart Tool or a Dangerous Shortcut?

Written by: Nur Aisyah binti Rahman

In recent years, artificial intelligence (AI) has become a familiar part of students' daily learning. Although it provides quick support, depending on it without thinking can create serious problems. This article examines why students use AI, its benefits and dangers, and the importance of using it responsibly.

To begin with, there are various reasons why students turn to AI for schoolwork. One reason is that it provides immediate explanations when lessons are difficult to understand. Instead of waiting for the next class, students can ask follow-up questions until a topic becomes clearer. For instance, AI can explain a confusing grammar rule using examples suited to their level. Another reason is that it can independent revision. Students can request practice quizzes or organise a study plan based on topics they need to improve. Consequently, they may use their study time more .

Furthermore, AI offers several benefits, but it also presents certain dangers. One benefit is that it can make revision more effective. For example, AI can practice questions and provide on a student's first draft. This allows learners to identify their weaknesses before the next lesson. However, one danger is that AI can students by presenting information confidently. A student who copies a false fact into an assignment may lose marks and misunderstand the topic. Over time, depending too heavily on AI may also weaken their ability to think .

Finally, students can use AI responsibly by developing several practical habits. One approach is to check the of its answers before using them. Students should compare important information with textbooks, teacher notes or trusted websites. For instance, an AI-generated historical date should be checked against another trustworthy source. Another approach is to maintain honesty by using AI as a guide rather than a replacement for their own work. Students may request ideas or , but they should write their final answers themselves. As a result, they can enjoy the advantages of technology while strengthening their own skills.

All things considered, AI is a valuable learning tool when students use it wisely and responsibly. It is hoped that more teenagers will learn to question AI responses instead of accepting them immediately. Therefore, teachers and parents should encourage students to check information carefully and produce their own work.
